David is a final-year MEng Robotics and AI student at University College London. His research interests include robot learning, vision-language-action models, and world-action models. He is interested in how robots can better understand human intentions, anticipate the outcomes of their actions, and adapt to new tasks and environments. His goal is to enable more intuitive and effective human–robot interaction in everyday settings.
